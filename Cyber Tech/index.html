STAY SAFE ONLINE

Cybersecurity Awareness Tips

Small security habits can make a significant difference in protecting your digital life.

Use Strong Passwords

Create long, unique passwords and use a trusted password manager.

Recognize Phishing

Check senders, links, and unexpected attachments before interacting.

Enable 2FA

Use multi-factor authentication to add another layer of account protection.

Browse Safely

Use trusted websites, check URLs, and keep your browser updated.

Secure Public Wi-Fi

Avoid sensitive transactions on untrusted networks and verify hotspots.

Understand Social Engineering

Be cautious of urgent requests for passwords, codes, or private information.

Update Your Software

Install security updates to reduce exposure to known vulnerabilities.

Protect Personal Data

Share less personal information and review your account privacy settings.

HAVE QUESTIONS?

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about cybersecurity and getting started.

What is cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computers, networks, applications, and information from unauthorized access, damage, and digital attacks.

What is ethical hacking?

Ethical hacking is authorized security testing that helps identify and fix vulnerabilities before malicious attackers can exploit them.

Which cybersecurity tools should beginners learn?

Start with networking fundamentals and tools such as Wireshark, Nmap, and OWASP ZAP in safe learning environments.

Can students learn cybersecurity for free?

Yes. Platforms such as Cisco Networking Academy, freeCodeCamp, OWASP, and PortSwigger Academy offer free learning materials.

What is penetration testing?

Penetration testing is an authorized assessment that simulates attacks to identify security weaknesses in systems and applications.

Is Kali Linux suitable for beginners?

Beginners can explore Kali Linux, but learning Linux commands, networking, and security fundamentals first makes the experience easier.

Where can I practice cybersecurity legally?

Use dedicated environments such as TryHackMe, Hack The Box Academy, and PortSwigger Web Security Academy. Never test systems without permission.
